# MetroMove Demo Guide

## Purpose

This walkthrough demonstrates Practero’s current fixture-only milestone. MetroMove is fictional, every source is safe to publish, and every demo route carries a visible **Sample analysis** disclosure. Live GPT-5.6 analysis is not implemented.

## Recommended judge walkthrough

### 1. Establish the product promise

Open `/` and explain:

> Practero helps forward-deployed teams compare organizational intent, field reality, and technical reality—then turns the gaps into an evidence-backed deployment path.

Select **Try MetroMove Demo**.

### 2. Read the engagement signal

On `/demo/metromove`, point out:

- the clear sample-data disclosure;
- the deterministic readiness status and score;
- five evidence sources across all three realities;
- six open Reality Gaps; and
- four linked deployment workstreams.

The readiness score is a transparent policy result, not a model opinion.

### 3. Inspect the source material

Open **Evidence** and briefly inspect:

1. MetroMove pilot management requirements;
2. Driver discovery interview notes; and
3. Current architecture and feature summary.

On a source page, show the stable source ID, fixture content ID, chunk count, complete text, and traceability usage count.

### 4. Show the structured Reality Map

Open **Reality Map**. Expand supporting excerpts for:

- continuous ride updates;
- connectivity loss;
- the online-only ride-state capability; and
- unresolved cancellation or escalation questions.

Emphasize that unresolved evidence remains labeled unresolved.

### 5. Open the centerpiece gap

Open **Reality Gaps**, then select **Continuous ride updates conflict with field connectivity**.

The detail view aligns:

- organizational intent: continuous ride-state visibility is required;
- field reality: drivers report ten-to-twenty-minute coverage gaps; and
- technical reality: the product has no local queue or reconnect synchronization.

Each card contains an exact fixture excerpt and a link back to the complete source. The gap’s Critical severity is derived from impact, likelihood, deployment criticality, and mitigation inputs.

### 6. Follow evidence into action

Review the connectivity gap’s operational and technical impact, recommended action, and acceptance criterion. Select **Open Deployment Path**.

In **Reliable ride-state delivery**, show:

- prioritized implementation tasks;
- the operations decision required;
- the exact acceptance criterion for a simulated 15-minute outage;
- event-ordering risk and mitigation; and
- the blocked pilot checklist item.

Briefly scan the onboarding, transaction-control, and support workstreams to show dependencies and stakeholder actions.

### 7. Finish with the decision view

Open **Executive Brief** and summarize:

- overall readiness;
- the critical connectivity contradiction;
- decisions required; and
- recommended next steps.

Close by reiterating that the current experience is validated sample analysis. Firebase persistence and live GPT-5.6 analysis are later milestones.

## Suggested two-to-three-minute pacing

| Time | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Landing page and the three realities |
| 0:25–0:50 | MetroMove readiness and evidence coverage |
| 0:50–1:15 | One complete source and Reality Map excerpt |
| 1:15–2:05 | Connectivity gap and exact three-world evidence |
| 2:05–2:35 | Deployment workstream and acceptance criterion |
| 2:35–3:00 | Executive Brief and milestone disclosure |

## Demo safeguards

- Do not describe the sample analysis as a live model result.
- Do not imply that authentication, persistence, upload, or live extraction exists.
- If asked about data provenance, open an evidence source and show the exact referenced sentence.
- If asked about scores, explain the deterministic severity and readiness policy.
- No account, API key, Firebase project, or external service is required for this milestone.
